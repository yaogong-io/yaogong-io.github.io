Research Projects

MeshCraft: Text-Guided 3D Mesh Painting
Master’s Thesis, University of Pennsylvania

My master’s thesis explored text-guided coloring of 3D meshes while preserving their geometry. I developed a neural-field pipeline combining differentiable rendering, CLIP guidance, and region-weighted color blending, and evaluated it through qualitative examples.
